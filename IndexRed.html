Ourselves

Super Bold Events PLT is a Kuala Lumpur–based event management outfit for people who don't want a safe event. We plan the logistics with total precision so you can take the creative risk — the unexpected venue, the guest list mash-up, the run of show nobody's tried before — and still hit every cue on time.
